Glowbound Support

Send glowing sparks around the loop and light up every picture.

Need help, found a bug or have an idea? Send us a message and include your iPhone model and iOS version. We usually reply within a few days.

How do I play?

Tap a spark in one of the columns. It rides once around the loop and, at every row and column it passes, lights the nearest dark cells of its own colour (lit cells let it see through). A spark that uses all its charges burns out; one with charges left waits in the dock. Every tap costs a move. You lose if you run out of moves or a spark comes back to a full dock, so plan the order: light the outer colours first. Choose Easy, Medium or Hard on the main screen; each keeps its own level number.

I bought "Remove Ads" but still see ads

Open the game, go to Settings (gear icon) or the Shop and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy. The optional reward videos ("Extra dock slot", extra hints and "Double coins") stay available on purpose. They only play when you tap them.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

In Europe, the UK and Switzerland, open Settings → Ad privacy choices in the game. On any iPhone you can also go to iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

The leaderboard doesn't show my score

Leaderboards use Game Center. Make sure you are signed in under iOS Settings → Game Center. Your score is sent each time you light a picture; the weekly board resets every Monday.

Will I lose my progress?

Levels, stars, coins and themes are saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.
