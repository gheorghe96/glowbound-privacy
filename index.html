Glowbound Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Short version: Glowbound has no accounts and we don't run any servers. Your progress stays on your device. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, which may use device data to show and measure ads. You choose whether ads can be personalised, and you can change that choice at any time.

Who we are

Glowbound is an iOS game published by Gheorghe Curcă ("we", "us"). We are responsible for the data practices described here. Contact details are at the end of this page.

Data stored on your device

Your picture progress on each difficulty, stars, coins, hints, colour themes, daily missions, login streak and settings (such as sound and vibration) are saved only on your device. We do not receive or store them. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep Glowbound free, it shows ads using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob). When ads load, Google and its advertising partners may collect and process:

  • device identifiers, such as the Advertising Identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking, and the identifier for vendors (IDFV);
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version);
  • ad interactions (ads shown, taps, completed rewarded videos) and diagnostic data about ad performance.

This data is used to show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and, if you agree, personalise ads. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choices about ads

  • Europe (EEA), UK and Switzerland: on first launch the game shows a consent form from Google. You can accept, refuse or choose purposes and partners. To change your choice later, open Settings → Ad privacy choices in the game.
  • Tracking on iOS: the game asks for permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the IDFA is not shared and ads are not personalised using it. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Remove Ads: the optional one-time "Remove Ads" purchase turns off the ads shown between pictures. Optional rewarded videos (for example, an extra dock slot or extra hints) remain available and are only shown when you tap them.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the number of pictures you light is submitted to Glowbound's Game Center leaderboards, and the game shows your rank and your friends' scores. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy; we do not receive your Apple ID.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. To unlock and restore "Remove Ads", the game uses Apple's StoreKit and may use RevenueCat, a purchase-management service. RevenueCat receives your purchase history (product, date, price and country of the store) and an anonymous ID generated for this app install. It does not receive your name, email or Apple ID. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

Children

Glowbound is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the ads in the game, contact us and we will help.

Legal bases and your rights

Where EU/UK law applies, personalised advertising relies on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in the game's settings. Non-personalised ads, ad measurement and fraud prevention rely on legitimate interests. You have the right to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to processing; for data held by Google, you can also use My Ad Center. You may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Retention

We keep no personal data ourselves. Google and Apple keep data according to their own policies linked above.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
